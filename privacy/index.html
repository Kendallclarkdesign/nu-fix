Privacy Policy

What we collect when you visit this site or ask for a quote, what we do with it, and your choices. Last updated November 7, 2026.

Who we are

Nu-Fix, LLC provides building maintenance and restoration work, including roofing, facade, siding, windows, waterproofing, flooring, and concrete, in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Delaware. Our mailing address is 132 Veterans Lane, Unit A PMB122, Doylestown, PA 18901. You can reach us at service@nu-fix.com or 267.640.1778.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out a quote or contact form, you may give us your name, phone number, email address, property address, the kind of work you need, and a message. When you call or email us, we keep what you tell us so we can follow up.

Information from your visit. Like most websites, ours and our hosting provider record basic visit information such as pages viewed, the type of device and browser, and the date and time. We also use the Meta Pixel, described below.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, schedule a visit, and send you a written quote.
  • To keep records of the work we quote and do.
  • To see which of our ads and pages lead to quote requests, so we can spend our advertising money well.
  • To keep this website working and secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

Meta Pixel and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram). It uses cookies and similar tools to record when you view a page on this site, send a quote form, or tap our phone number. Meta receives that activity along with technical details such as your browser and IP address. We use it to measure how our ads perform and to show our ads to people who may be interested in our services. Meta handles that data under its own privacy policy.

If you submit a lead form directly on Facebook or Instagram, the information you enter is shared with us, and Meta handles it under its own terms.

To limit this, you can turn off ad personalization in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings, block cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser privacy tool. The site still works if you do.

Who else sees your information

We share information only with the services that help us run the business: our website host (Netlify, which processes form submissions), Meta (through the Pixel, as described above), and our email provider. They may only use it to provide their service to us. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and job records for as long as we need them to respond to you and keep normal business and tax records. After that, we delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email service@nu-fix.com or call 267.640.1778. Some states give residents specific privacy rights, and we will honor those requests as the law requires.

Children

This site is for property owners and managers. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
