What We Handle for Commercial & Institutional Clients

Nu-Fix supports property managers, facility directors, and building owners with the same building maintenance work we do for homeowners, scaled and documented for institutional needs. That includes flat and low-slope roof systems, facade and cladding, window repair and replacement, flooring, concrete and pavement, and waterproofing for below-grade and exterior wall assemblies.

We also handle recurring preventative maintenance contracts, so inspections and minor repairs happen on schedule instead of becoming reactive work orders.
